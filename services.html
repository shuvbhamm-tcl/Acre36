What Acre36 handles —
and what we hand to specialists.

One advisor, five situations. We coordinate the parts of a land decision that are hard to do alone, and we're upfront about where a lawyer, registrar or surveyor takes over.

01 — Advisory

Land & Property Advisory

Buying, selling or investing in agricultural, residential or commercial land. We start with your goal and horizon, then narrow to opportunities worth a real look — not a scroll of listings.

02 — Diligence

Land Evaluation & Due Diligence

Document review, ownership and record checks, land-use considerations, risk identification and site verification — coordinated with qualified legal professionals where required.

03 — Strategy

Market & Investment Advisory

City and micro-market selection based on growth potential, infrastructure and price trends — matched to how long you actually plan to hold.

04 — Commercial

Commercial Land Advisory

Land requirements for businesses, developers, industrial and institutional users — including zoning, access and utility considerations.

05 — Execution

Site & Transaction Assistance

Site visits, local coordination, negotiation support, documentation coordination and transaction assistance — from shortlist through to registration.

A note on scope

We coordinate. We don't replace your lawyer.

Acre36 advises on and coordinates the land decision — market understanding, evaluation, negotiation and logistics. For registration, legal opinions and statutory compliance, we work alongside qualified legal professionals and never position ourselves as a substitute for one.
