We started Acre36 because the market was easy to buy into and hard to understand.

Chhattisgarh's land market has real opportunity in it. What it lacked was a source people could trust to explain it honestly — including the parts that don't favour a quick sale.

Acre36 team, site visit — Durg

Who We Are

A local advisory network, not a listings company.

Acre36 is a Chhattisgarh land consultant and advisory network, built for people evaluating or investing in land here — especially those based outside the state without a trusted local contact. We're not trying to out-list 99acres or Housing. We're trying to be the place that actually explains the market before you commit to it.

Our advisors visit the sites we write about. Our reports carry an author and an update date. And our first honest answer to "is this a good investment?" is usually "it depends" — followed by the specifics that make it true.

What We Stand For

Four commitments that shape everything we publish.

Evidence over claims

Every report explains where its information comes from. If we haven't verified something, we say so.

Honest about risk

We flag what needs additional verification. A market being "hot" doesn't mean every parcel in it is safe.

People, not personas

The advisors you speak to are the people behind the research — not a separate sales layer.

Scope, clearly stated

We coordinate the land decision. We're not a substitute for your lawyer, registrar or surveyor.
